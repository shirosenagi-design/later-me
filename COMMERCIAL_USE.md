# Commercial Use Policy

Later, Me. is source-available, not open source. Noncommercial rights in the
software are governed by the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

Commercial use is not automatically licensed under the repository license. Any
commercial use requires prior written permission from the creator, 鮫洲ネオ.
This document explains the creator's general permission philosophy; it does not
itself grant commercial rights.

## Permission approach

The permission requirement is not intended as a licensing-fee scheme.
Permission may be granted free of charge, including to commercial projects,
when the creator considers the intended use consistent with the purpose and
principles of Later, Me.

A project is not rejected merely because it is commercial. Derivative works,
improved implementations, and commercial successors are not inherently
discouraged. A genuinely improved implementation aligned with the project's
goals may be welcomed.

Prior review is mainly intended to avoid uses that turn human consideration
into manipulation, including:

- intentionally encouraging emotional dependency for monetization;
- gamified intimacy, relationship scores, affection levels, or streaks;
- paid closeness or paid intimacy;
- manipulative engagement loops;
- aggressive or unwanted AI calling; and
- using apparent care primarily to maximize retention, sales, or repeated
  contact.

These examples describe the creator's general permission philosophy. They are
not an automatic or machine-enforced license test. The legal rule remains that
commercial use requires separate prior written permission.

Participation in the CALL-E Hackathon and judging or evaluation access are
governed separately by the Hackathon Official Rules.
